If your team already has web code worth reusing, `HybridWebView` offers a way to host it in a .NET MAUI app and talk to it from C#. Eilon Lipton explains the experiment and walks through a demo.

Before that, Maddy and David cover blogs and community projects, including FFImageLoading.Compat, AppleDev Tools, badge counters, speech to text, keyboard extensions, and Mobile Blazor Bindings.
