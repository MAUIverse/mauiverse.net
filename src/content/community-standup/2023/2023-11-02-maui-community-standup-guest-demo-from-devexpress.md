After the community links and announcements, the second half of the show goes to DevExpress. Their demo walks through project templates and getting started, then moves through controls including the collection view, data grid, charts, scheduler, image editor, HTML editor, and PDF viewer.

The session ends with a mention of the .NET Meteor and DotRush community tools for VS Code.
