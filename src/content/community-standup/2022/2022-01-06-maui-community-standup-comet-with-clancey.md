Not everyone wants to write XAML and bind to a view model. James Clancey, the driving force behind Comet, shows how a Model-View-Update approach lets you describe a .NET MAUI UI in plain C#.

The episode opens with a look at the .NET MAUI Preview 11 release and the community's holiday projects, including a fun Korean-number-system clock app written with Comet. It's worth a watch if you're curious about declarative UI options on top of .NET MAUI.
