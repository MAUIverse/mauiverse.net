On-device inferencing keeps data on the phone, works offline, and avoids a round trip to the cloud. Mike Parker explains how the ONNX Runtime fits into a Xamarin.Forms app and walks through using it for machine learning tasks.

Maddy and David pair the demo with their usual roundup of community links, so the episode doubles as a catch-up on .NET MAUI news from the month.
