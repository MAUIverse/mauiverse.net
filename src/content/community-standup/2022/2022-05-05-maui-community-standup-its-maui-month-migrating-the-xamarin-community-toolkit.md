The final standup before the general release focuses on a question many library authors were asking: how do you move a popular Xamarin library to .NET MAUI?

Brandon Minnick shares what he and the Xamarin Community Toolkit team learned while getting their packages ready. Maddy and David add the month's community links and a bit of launch-month excitement.
