After a couple of months off, the maintainers are back with a lighter agenda focused on .NET 10 and a few pull requests worth watching.

- **.NET 10 updates** — Brandon shares where the Toolkit stands as .NET 10 approaches, and Vlad shares his .NET Foundation candidacy.
- **Package validation** — James brings up enabling package validation in the Toolkit.
- **`CameraView` on Windows** — a PR that fixes the crash when switching cameras.
- **`MediaElement` full screen events** and **Android service updates** — two more PRs on the agenda.

The standup closes with the community Q&A.
