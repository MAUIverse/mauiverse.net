This one starts with release news and a team change, then moves into a long list of proposals.

- **Releases** — `CommunityToolkit.Maui` 5.0.0, `CommunityToolkit.Maui.MediaElement` 1.0.2, and `CommunityToolkit.Maui.Markup` 3.1.0
- **Team update** — Pedro announces he's stepping back from the core maintainer team, and the team asks for new maintainers
- **Proposals** — a keyboard manager, `CameraView`, `TouchEffect`, and a `PopupService`
- **PRs and discussions** — Windows maps, NuGet versioning for PR builds, and being less aggressive about disposing controls
- **Markup** — adding typed bindings to the gesture extensions
