Last month the Toolkit held its first election to add maintainers while keeping the project community-led. This standup welcomes the results.

Brandon introduces the new team members, who each share their background and how they hope to contribute. The agenda then turns to the `PopupService` PR and a Markup PR that adds C# Hot Reload support. The session ends with the community Q&A.
