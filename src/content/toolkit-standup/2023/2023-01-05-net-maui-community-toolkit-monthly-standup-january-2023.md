The first standup of 2023 works through a full agenda for both `CommunityToolkit.Maui` and `CommunityToolkit.Maui.Markup`, with a running conversation about the status of `MediaElement` and its Tizen support.

- **Issues** — `Expander` inside a `ScrollView` breaking on iOS, and an analyzer for `.UseMauiCommunityToolkit()`
- **Proposals** — `TouchEffect`, plus ideas for dialogs and automatically closing `needs reproduction` issues after five days
- **Discussions** — how aggressively controls should dispose, and nightly NuGet builds
- **Markup** — extending `.Bind()` to support typed bindings, and using app styles for theming
