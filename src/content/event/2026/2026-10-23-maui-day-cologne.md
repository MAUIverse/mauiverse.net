MAUI Day is a one-day, in-person event filled with sessions that have to do with cross-platform development using, you guessed it, .NET MAUI. 

Its also free! 

Speakers gathered at the Microsoft office in Cologne, Germany on October 23rd, 2026 to present about the latest and greatest in the .NET MAUI ecosystem.

The day covered:

- **Building the Next Generation of Apps with .NET MAUI:** Gerald Versluis on where MAUI stands today and what .NET 11 brings
- **.NET MAUI 11: Focused on Perf:** Simon Rozsival on CoreCLR as the default runtime, Native AOT, startup profiles and trimming
- **Bulletproof .NET MAUI:** Michał Pobuta on building apps that don't crash
- **Building with AI at the Edge:** David Ortinau on building efficiently with the latest tools and services
- **One Screen, Every Device:** Almir Vuk on state triggers and the Visual State Manager
- **Massively distributed live coding with AI:** Stéphane Delcroix and the MAUI team live coding an app
- **.NET MAUI Q&A Panel:** the MAUI team answering questions
