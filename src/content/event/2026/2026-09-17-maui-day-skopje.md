MAUI Day is a one-day, in-person event filled with sessions that have to do with cross-platform development using, you guessed it, .NET MAUI. 

Its also free! 

Speakers gathered at INNOFEIT EDIH in Skopje, North Macedonia on September 17th, 2026 to present about the latest and greatest in the .NET MAUI ecosystem.

The day covered:

- **Building Your First .NET MAUI App:** a four-hour hands-on workshop with Gerald Versluis
- **The Secret Life of a MAUI Page:** Mihajlo Mladenov on page construction, handlers, navigation and why some pages stay in memory
- **Offline AI Agents:** Mario Naumoski on building efficient, fully offline agents with .NET MAUI, Semantic Kernel and small language models
- **XAML C# Expressions and Hot Reload:** Stéphane Delcroix on the XAML improvements arriving in .NET 11
- **Bulletproof .NET MAUI:** Michał Pobuta on building apps that don't crash, and knowing when they do
