MAUI Day is a one-day, in-person event filled with sessions that have to do with cross-platform development using, you guessed it, .NET MAUI. 

Its also free! 

Speakers gathered at Politechnika Krakowska in Kraków, Poland on May 29th, 2026 to present about the latest and greatest in the .NET MAUI ecosystem.

The day covered:

- **.NET MAUI Now and Next:** David Ortinau on the product highlights and the vision beyond .NET 10
- **Apple Watch:** Damian Antonowicz on building a native Swift watch app that works with .NET MAUI
- **Performance Hacks:** Łukasz Grudziński on handling heavy layouts when best practices aren't enough
- **AI-Assisted PRs:** Jakub Florkowski and Gerald Versluis on agentic workflows for code quality
- **Intelligent apps:** Codrina Merigo on adding AI capabilities to a MAUI app
- **Observability in .NET MAUI 10:** Stefan Pölz on logs, metrics and traces with OpenTelemetry, Aspire and Sentry
- **Aspire for your .NET MAUI app:** Mark Allibone on improving the developer experience for mobile apps with backends
- **XAML C# Expressions:** Stéphane Delcroix on the XAML improvements arriving in .NET 11
- **The MAUI Panel:** the MAUI team answering questions
