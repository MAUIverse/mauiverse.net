.NET MAUI left preview with Visual Studio 2022 17.3. Edin Kapić's InfoQ article captures the single-project model, tooling highlights, and the community's mix of excitement and bug-count concerns at launch.

## What you'll learn

- **Single-project MAUI** — shared UI/code with platform asset folders under one .NET project
- **VS 2022 tooling** — XAML Live Preview, Hot Reload, and IntelliCode for MAUI workflows
- **Platform support at GA** — Android, iOS, macOS (Catalyst), and Windows targets under .NET 6
- **Migration context** — Xamarin.Forms still supported until May 2024, with VS guidance for moving iOS/Android projects

Read the full piece for the long preview path to GA and how Microsoft positioned MAUI for new work.
