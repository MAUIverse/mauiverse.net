Sam Basu's *Sands of MAUI* #58 is a curated stop for developers tracking .NET MAUI Crash Course, .NET MAUI Courseware, .NET MAUI Modes, .NET MAUI Content. It brings together timely resources from the .NET MAUI ecosystem, with enough context to help you decide which original posts, videos, and announcements to open next.

## In this issue

- **.NET MAUI Crash Course** — .NET MAUI getting ready for General Availability (GA) and runtime/tooling stabilizing, there are a lot of developers wanting to learn. .NET MAUI, one has to break down things to the basics—what is the runtime, the promise, how to get started and how to be productive building cross-platform mobile/desktop apps?
- **.NET MAUI Courseware** — Are you the type of developer who learns best by doing things? .NET MAUI—a full Microsoft Learn courseware on building cross-platform mobile/desktop apps . .NET MAUI and more advanced topics such as local data storage or invoking REST-based web services.
- **.NET MAUI Modes** — Most mobile/desktop users have strong preferences on how their screen lights up with content—this is commonly driven by light or dark modes. The system theme is not only a popular preference, but also important to users with accessibility needs.
- **.NET MAUI Content** — One of the best indicators of a framework's popularity amongst developers is community involvement and the amount of content being created. The Xamarin ecosystem has always been great—Steven Thewissen had done the awesome Xamarin UI July back in 2019.
- **.NET MAUI @ Techorama** — .NET MAUI and live in Europe? As developer conferences come back to in-person shows, there is a big one on the horizon— Techorama BE is happening May 23-25 in Antwerp Belgium. Techorama promises three days of wonderful developer content with speakers and attendees from all over.
