Migrating a Xamarin app doesn't have to mean a rewrite. At .NET Conf 2022, Sweeky Satpathy breaks down exactly what changes when moving libraries and UI to .NET MAUI, and where tooling can help.

## What you'll learn

- **Library-level changes** — what needs to move or adapt when leaving Xamarin behind
- **UI migration considerations** — adjusting views and layouts for .NET MAUI
- **The .NET Upgrade Assistant** — how the tool automates parts of the migration path
- **Planning the move** — a realistic sense of scope before you start

Watch the full session for the concrete steps to take your Xamarin app to .NET MAUI.
