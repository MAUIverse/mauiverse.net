Accessibility is easy to treat as a later-stage concern, but Rachel Kang's .NET Conf 2022 session makes the case that it's always the right time to start — and shows the .NET MAUI tooling that makes it practical.

## What you'll learn

- **Accessibility APIs in .NET MAUI** — what's available out of the box for making apps usable by everyone
- **Recent improvements** — what's new for accessibility support across platforms
- **Tools that help** — ways to check and improve accessibility without guesswork
- **Starting at any stage** — how to bring accessibility into an app whether you're beginning or well underway

Watch the full talk for the walkthrough of APIs and tools you can apply to your own .NET MAUI app today.
