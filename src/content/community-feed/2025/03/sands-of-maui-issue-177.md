Sam Basu's *Sands of MAUI* #177 is a curated stop for developers tracking Performance Improvements, .NET 10 Preview, Copilot Agent, Blazor Authentication. It brings together timely resources from the .NET MAUI ecosystem, with enough context to help you decide which original posts, videos, and announcements to open next.

## In this issue

- **Performance Improvements** — .NET cross-platform development stack, allowing developers to reach mobile and desktop form factors from a single shared codebase. .NET runtime. .NET 9 . .NET MAUI focuses on two key things—full app trimming and NativeAOT runtime.
- **.NET 10 Preview** — .NET is powerful, open-source, cross-platform and welcoming to all, with mature tooling accompanied by rich ecosystems. .NET settling on a yearly cadence, there are fresh new bits for developers every November—the work starts early in the year though.
- **Copilot Agent** — .NET developers to infuse apps with solutions powered by generative AI and large/small language models. AI is also an opportunity to streamline and automate developer workflows for better productivity. The days of simple Generative AI seem already behind us.
- **Blazor Authentication** — .NET developers to create cross-platform apps for Android, iOS, macOS and Windows, with deep platform integrations, native UI and hybrid web experiences. .NET runtime means Blazor UI/styles/navigation can drive experiences on web and native apps.
- **Industrial UX** — Modern web, mobile and desktop apps often strive for delightful UX, and thoughtful UI design is one way to achieve the goal. However, there has traditionally been some friction in the designer-developer handoff—it helps to understand some basic tenets of good UX.
