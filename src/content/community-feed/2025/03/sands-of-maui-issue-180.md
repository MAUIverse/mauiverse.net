Sam Basu's *Sands of MAUI* #180 is a curated stop for developers tracking .NET 10 Preview 2, .NET MAUI Toolkit Alerts, Blazor Spreadsheet Madness, .NET Developer Mistakes. It brings together timely resources from the .NET MAUI ecosystem, with enough context to help you decide which original posts, videos, and announcements to open next.

## In this issue

- **.NET 10 Preview 2** — .NET is powerful, open-source, cross-platform and welcoming to all, with mature tooling accompanied by rich ecosystems. .NET settling on a yearly cadence, there are fresh new bits for developers every November—the work starts early in the year though.
- **.NET MAUI Toolkit Alerts** — .NET cross-platform development stack, allowing developers to reach mobile and desktop form factors from a single shared codebase. Users do expect modern apps to have solid UX. Mobile apps, in particular, need to have quick interactivity to keep users engaged and informed.
- **Blazor Spreadsheet Madness** — Spring is in the air for much of the world, and that means March Madness in the US—the couple of weeks everyone suddenly cares about basketball. The real competition for sports enthusiasts is with tournament brackets, and Rick Hellwege wrote up a wonderful article bringing in the March Madness fun with Blazor Spreadsheet UI .
- **.NET Developer Mistakes** — .NET developers to create cross-platform apps for Android, iOS, macOS and Windows, with deep platform integrations, native UI and hybrid web experiences. .NET code—the basics have to be right.
- **Progress @ MVP Summit** — For Microsoft MVPs/RDs, the MVP Summit is a weeklong gathering of hardcore Microsoft tech enthusiasts—folks from all over the world get together on Redmond campus or online.
