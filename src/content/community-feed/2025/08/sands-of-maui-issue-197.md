- **SkiaSharp in .NET MAUI** — .NET cross-platform development stack, allowing developers to reach mobile and desktop form factors from a single shared codebase. .NET MAUI have a lot of ammunition at their disposal—the built-in framework abstractions and libraries like Progress Telerik UI, make a vast amount of UI available that works seamlessly across platforms.
