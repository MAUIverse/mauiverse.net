Sam Basu's *Sands of MAUI* #172 is a curated stop for developers tracking State of .NET, Document Processing, .NET 9 Updates, Easy APIs. It brings together timely resources from the .NET MAUI ecosystem, with enough context to help you decide which original posts, videos, and announcements to open next.

## In this issue

- **State of .NET** — .NET 9 last November. .NET technology stack, and, as enterprises plot modernization, it is always good to provide developer perspective. .NET 9 for app modernization. .NET, with an eye toward AI integrations. .NET Aspire and more, along with tooling updates and performance tuning.
- **Document Processing** — .NET developers to create cross-platform apps for Android, iOS macOS and Windows, with deep native integrations, platform-native UI and hybrid experiences.
- **.NET 9 Updates** — .NET is powerful, open-source, cross-platform and welcoming to all, with mature tooling accompanied by rich ecosystems. .NET 9, developers had a lot to learn. .NET 9 . .NET most relevant to them. .NET runtime, libraries and SDKs, along with corresponding tooling changes.
- **Easy APIs** — .NET cross-platform development stack, allowing developers to reach mobile and desktop form factors from a single shared codebase. Modern mobile/desktop apps do not, however, live in a silo—any real-world app depends on services outside the app.
- **Swetugg** — .NET is a large ecosystem with plenty for developers to learn and stay up on top of. And for developers to be inspired and learning from peers, nothing beats an in-person tech conference—wonderful things happen when passionate folks exchange ideas.
