Matt Goldman and Liam Elliott sit down with Brandon Minnick — AWS Developer Advocate, former Xamarin and Microsoft engineer, and lead maintainer of the .NET MAUI Community Toolkit — for a conversation that ranges from serverless .NET to Xamarin migrations to what makes async/await easy to get wrong.

## What you'll learn

- **Serverless .NET on AWS** — Native AOT, cold starts, and running ASP.NET Core on Lambda
- **Xamarin to .NET MAUI migrations** — Brandon's perspective from maintaining the Community Toolkit through the transition
- **The Community Toolkit and its markup library** — where XAML gives way to a C# markup approach
- **Breaking changes in OSS** — lessons from Rx.NET on managing them without alienating users
- **Async/await best practices** — including why async void keeps catching people out
- **Brandon's own projects** — GitTrends, Punday, and his upcoming .NET MAUI course

Listen to the full episode for the beer pairing and the full tour of AWS, MAUI, and async pitfalls.
