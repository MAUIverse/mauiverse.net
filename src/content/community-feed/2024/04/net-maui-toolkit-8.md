Community Toolkit 8 delivers TouchBehavior — the MAUI successor to Xamarin's TouchEffect — plus a Snackbar rewrite. Edin Kapić summarizes the headline features and the Windows Snackbar setup gotcha.

## What you'll learn

- **TouchBehavior** — declarative touch/mouse interaction on visual elements without subclassing
- **Snackbar rewrite** — including a Windows packaging/`MauiAppBuilder` setup step
- **Android navigation bar styling** — color and light/dark style controls
- **Popup reliability** — size, position, gesture, and window-detection fixes across platforms

Read the full InfoQ article for migration context as Xamarin approached end of support.
