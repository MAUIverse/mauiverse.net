- **.NET 9 Update** — .NET is constantly evolving and the next big milestone is already cooking. .NET 9 with Preview 7 now out . .NET Libraries, Runtime and SDKs. .NET 9 focuses on client apps, cloud-native apps and intelligent app development.
- **VS Code Extensions** — .NET cross-platform development stack, allowing developers to reach mobile and desktop form factors from single shared codebase. .NET MAUI—many seeking consistent building experiences from Windows/macOS/Linux developer machines.
