.NET MAUI 9 shipped with new controls, partner samples, and a serious Apple list/carousel rewrite. Edin Kapić's InfoQ roundup covers what landed at GA and what to migrate carefully.

## What you'll learn

- **HybridWebView** — host HTML/JS/CSS with a message bridge between web and .NET
- **TitleBar** — custom window title bars on Windows (Mac Catalyst later)
- **Syncfusion free controls** — 14 partner controls showcased in the updated app template
- **Apple CollectionView/CarouselView** — handler reimplementation that needs a `MauiProgram` opt-in
- **Deprecations** — `Frame` → `Border`, and `Application.MainPage` giving way to `Window.Page`

Read the full post for the preview-to-GA timeline and early SR feedback after launch.
