Community Toolkit 9 is mostly fixes and a breaking AlertView change — with CameraView arriving via a parallel package shortly after. Edin Kapić's InfoQ article separates the main release from those out-of-band NuGets.

## What you'll learn

- **CompareConverter made generic** — cleaner XAML comparisons against `IComparable` values
- **Breaking AlertView change** — `FrozenSet` replaced by `IReadOnlyList` on iOS
- **CameraView package** — Xamarin-ported camera control shipping as a separate NuGet for faster iteration
- **MediaElement updates** — lock-screen support and bug fixes in the companion package

Read the full write-up for the 9.0.1 patch notes and Microsoft Learn documentation links.
