When Xamarin was officially archived, Pieter Nijs wrote a personal look back at a tool that shaped his career. He recalls discovering Xamarin as a .NET developer who wanted to build native apps with his existing C# knowledge, and the excitement when Xamarin.Forms arrived. It led to complex production apps, conference talks from local meetups to Techorama and Xamarin Expert Day in Cologne, and his place in the community.

The second half looks forward. He sees .NET MAUI as an expansion of Xamarin's ideas, built on modern .NET, and doesn't pretend that migrating will be painless. For teams committed to .NET, he argues it's still the logical next step.

It's an opinion piece rather than a how-to, and it ends with an invitation to share your own Xamarin memories and migration plans, along with links to Microsoft's migration documentation.
