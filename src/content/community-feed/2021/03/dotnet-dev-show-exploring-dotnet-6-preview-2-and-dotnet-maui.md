- **Release scope** — the controls, APIs, and platform work included in this milestone
- **Developer workflow** — the SDK, workload, and tooling changes that affect day-to-day .NET MAUI development
- **Compatibility** — what existing projects need to adopt, migrate, or test before moving forward
- **Platform progress** — how the release advances Android, iOS, macOS, and Windows support
