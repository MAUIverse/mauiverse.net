Sam Basu's first *Sands of MAUI* roundup captures the early .NET 6 Preview 2 era, when .NET MAUI was beginning to consolidate native mobile and desktop development. It is a useful historical guide to the platform features, samples, migration experiments, and community feedback that informed the MAUI journey.

## In this issue

- **.NET 6 Preview 2 Drop** — See the early MAUI capabilities arriving with .NET 6 Preview 2, including Mac Catalyst targeting, a multi-targeted single project, `HostBuilder` startup support, and the first universal control handlers.
- **Updated MAUI samples on .NET 6** — Explore the refreshed iOS, Android, and single-project samples, along with the specific workloads, CLI tooling, and limited IDE support needed to run them at the time.
- **Xamarin Q1 2021 Survey** — Find the community feedback opportunity focused on Xamarin's transition to .NET MAUI and the developer experience in Visual Studio.
- **Tinkering with MAUI on .NET 6 Preview 2** — Follow Sam's hands-on Twitch exploration of early MAUI builds, including failed builds, working controls, and practical experimentation.
- **Updating from Xamarin.Forms to MAUI** — Read a real migration experience that moves a boilerplate Xamarin.Forms app onto the evolving .NET MAUI tooling.

Open the original roundup to follow the links, see the supporting examples, and explore the referenced community work in more depth.
