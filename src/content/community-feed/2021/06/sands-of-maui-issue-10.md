Sam Basu's *Sands of MAUI* #10 is a curated stop for developers tracking .NET MAUI Preview 4 Now Out, Exploring .NET MAUI Preview 4, Future of App Dev With .NET, Build 2021 Recap. It brings together timely resources from the .NET MAUI ecosystem, with enough context to help you decide which original posts, videos, and announcements to open next.

## In this issue

- **.NET MAUI Preview 4 Now Out** — .NET MAUI developers— Preview 4 is now out and David Ortinau was on point to lay out the details. .NET MAUI. To showcase the progress, the MAUI team designed and developed a simple yet beautiful weather app— Weather ’21 .
- **Exploring .NET MAUI Preview 4** — .NET MAUI Preview 4 bits . While everything is in Preview, it was reassuring to see tooling and build/deploy to simulators work as intended. What also got a little spotlight was a Demo App that shipped with the Early Preview of Telerik UI for MAUI .
- **Future of App Dev With .NET** — .NET, a modern open-source development platform for building apps for any OS with the best performance and productivity. .NET stack? .NET developer.
- **Build 2021 Recap** — Lance McCarthy was the guest on the latest CodeItLive ChatShow episode. Because it was Build week and Lance is such a geek, instead of the regular interview it ended up being a Build recap . Ed Charbeneau stopped lurking in the chatroom and hopped on live mid-stream, as one does.
- **.NET 6 Preview 4 ASP.NET Updates** — .NET Core development. .NET Hot Reload updates, C# 10 improvements, Blazor WebAssembly Ahead-Of-Time (AOT) compilation and lots of tooling updates. .NET MAUI developers are the new Blazor MAUI App Templates, as well as minimal APIs for hosting/routing for small microservices or HTTP APIs as backend services.
