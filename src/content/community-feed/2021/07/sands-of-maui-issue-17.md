Sam Basu's *Sands of MAUI* #17 is a curated stop for developers tracking .NET MAUI Preview 6, .NET MAUI Preview 6 in Detail, Update on Community Toolkit, Surfing in MAUI. It brings together timely resources from the .NET MAUI ecosystem, with enough context to help you decide which original posts, videos, and announcements to open next.

## In this issue

- **.NET MAUI Preview 6** — .NET MAUI Preview 6 is here. .NET MAUI vision and tooling is starting to come together. .NET MAUI are nicely streamlined now into maui , maui-mobile and maui-desktop workloads, while the maui-check tool continues to evolve to run additional validations on local development environment.
- **.NET MAUI Preview 6 in Detail** — .NET MAUI Preview 6 updates —from a developer’s perspective. Gerald covered what the developer experience would be with Preview 6 bits, with updated workloads and tooling—including the need for XCode 13 Beta 1 for targeting iOS/MacOS.
- **Update on Community Toolkit** — Forms developer? .NET MAUI Community Toolkit —still open source and community supported. .NET MAUI Community Toolkit—targeted for Aug 2021 for first preview. .NET MAUI–compatible versions of the Xamarin Community Toolkit to avoid breaking changes while porting apps.
- **Surfing in MAUI** — .NET MAUI in a single day? tv/codeitlive . .NET MAUI basics, what's new, engineering details, migrations and development pipelines. Lots of learning and chatting with experts—should be a fun day.
- **Merge Conflict Anniversary** — James Montemagno and the ever-colorful Frank Krueger host the popular Merge Conflict weekly developer podcast. Merge Conflict recently hit the 5-year mark —time flies when you are having fun. .NET/Xamarin developers.
