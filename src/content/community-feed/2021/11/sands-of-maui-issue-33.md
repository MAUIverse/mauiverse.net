Sam Basu's *Sands of MAUI* #33 is a curated stop for developers tracking .NET 6, .NET MAUI Preview 10, .NET Conf 2021 Keynote, Introduction to .NET MAUI. It brings together timely resources from the .NET MAUI ecosystem, with enough context to help you decide which original posts, videos, and announcements to open next.

## In this issue

- **.NET 6** — .NET 6 . .NET 6 is out in full glory and proudly carries the Long Term Support (LTS) badge. .NET 6 announcement post and the key point to take away is massive gains in performance. .NET apps to run on new frontiers.
- **.NET MAUI Preview 10** — .NET MAUI—Preview 10 is now out. .NET MAUI Preview 10 . The key to note here is how easy it is to get started. 0 version. .NET MAUI dependencies and mobile platform runtimes/SDKs/simulators are included with a simple checkbox.
- **.NET Conf 2021 Keynote** — .NET Conf . .NET Conf was held Nov 9-11 this year—2 days of awesome content from Microsoft folks, before a full 24 hours of non-stop livestream with passionate community speakers from all around the world. .NET ecosystem.
- **Introduction to .NET MAUI** — .NET MAUI . .NET MAUI Preview 10, including the ease of development with Windows Subsystem for Android. .NET MAUI is catching up fast. While Maddy may be a little uncomfortably fond of her Mac , Hot Reload (both XAML and C#) is starting to work just about everywhere.
- **C# 10** — .NET MAUI. .NET 6 and VS 2022 launch comes the next language update—welcome to C# 10. Kathleen Dollard wrote up the C# 10 announcement post —covering all the features that make your code run faster and be more prettier/expressive.
