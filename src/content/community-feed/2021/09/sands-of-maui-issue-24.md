Sam Basu's *Sands of MAUI* #24 is a curated stop for developers tracking .NET MAUI Development on MacOS, App Center for Xamarin.Forms, AndroidSDK Tools, XamExpert Day. It brings together timely resources from the .NET MAUI ecosystem, with enough context to help you decide which original posts, videos, and announcements to open next.

## In this issue

- **.NET MAUI Development on MacOS** — .NET MAUI is a big promise, and modern cross-platform tooling has lots of dependencies from various teams—VS engineering, XAML experience, MAUI folks and, of course, bits from Apple and Google. .NET MAUI tooling.
- **App Center for Xamarin.Forms** — With mobile app lifecycles, the real work often begins after shipping the app to Stores—continued monitoring of app health and providing value to users through updates. If your app has crashes, you want to know and potentially do something about them before bad reviews come in.
- **AndroidSDK Tools** — .NET MAUI. .NET CLI tool. The tool is helpful for managing Android SDK installs, creating/managing Android Virtual Devices (AVDs), execute Android Debug Bridge (ADB) commands and start/deploy to Android Emulators.
- **XamExpert Day** — Some passionate Xamarin folks in EU had started the XamExpertDay —an all-day conference focusing on all things awesome in the Xamarin technology stack. .NET MAUI. The speaker list includes all the usual troublemakers in the space and the event is entirely online with two parallel tracks.
- **Scroll Reveal in Xamarin.Forms** — A common UX paradigm on mobile apps is to scroll through a list and be able to see the item/detail underneath—this is actually a little tricky to implement. Trust seasoned Xamarin developer Charlin Agramonte to come up with an elegant solution for scroll reveal experience .

Open the original roundup to follow the links, see the supporting examples, and explore the referenced community work in more depth.
