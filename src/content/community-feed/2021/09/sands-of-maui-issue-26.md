Sam Basu's *Sands of MAUI* #26 is a curated stop for developers tracking .NET MAUI Preview 8 Review, .NET MAUI Preview 8 Hands-On, The Ultimate .NET MAUI Update, .NET MAUI Tooling. It brings together timely resources from the .NET MAUI ecosystem, with enough context to help you decide which original posts, videos, and announcements to open next.

## In this issue

- **.NET MAUI Preview 8 Review** — .NET RC1, and Gerald Versluis produced a 'Breaking News' style video covering all the updates. .NET MAUI, when needed, should not be a big deal. .NET MAUI tooling support in Visual Studio 2022 Preview 4— Hot Reload and XAML Live Previewer combine well to really tighten the developer inner loop.
- **.NET MAUI Preview 8 Hands-On** — .NET MAUI Preview 8 update . .NET MAUI apps. cs cleans up app bootstrapping. You can also see the familiar excitement about Hot Reload, both XAML and C#, now starting to work—this is a big deal, along with easier iOS device deployments.
- **The Ultimate .NET MAUI Update** — .NET MAUI podcast . .NET MAUI Preview 8 and tooling updates. .NET MAUI release and the Visual Studio 2022 Preview 4 installation/tooling changes. .NET Conf.
- **.NET MAUI Tooling** — .NET MAUI developers. .NET MAUI features being worked on—the community stepped up the guesswork to point out the awesome. Some of the cool stuff includes Shadows , Borders , Corners , new Layouts , Live Preview and more.
- **Xamarin.Forms Custom Controls** — Forms development is creating custom UI—an isolated component that is reusable throughout the app and even across a suite of apps for better UX consistency. Forms. These are real-world strategies that help with control usage, maintainability and adhere to standards of control API consumption.
