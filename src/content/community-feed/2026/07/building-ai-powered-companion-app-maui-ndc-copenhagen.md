Ever wondered how to integrate AI into your cross-platform apps? In this NDC Copenhagen 2026 talk, Gerald Versluis harnesses .NET MAUI to build a seamless experience across platforms and layers in AI to make the app smarter and more responsive.

## What you'll learn

- **Integrating AI into .NET MAUI** — wiring AI functionality into a single cross-platform codebase
- **Building a companion experience** — personalized recommendations, ideas, and assistance driven by the model
- **Practical, end-to-end scenarios** — from personalized book recommendations to road-trip plans and recipe ideas
- **A seamless cross-platform UX** — using .NET MAUI to deliver a consistent experience across devices
- **The full toolkit** — everything you need to go build your own "personal Jarvis"

Whether you're a .NET developer looking to modernize your apps or curious about adding intelligent features, this ~1 hour session offers a practical, demo-led starting point. Watch the full talk to follow the build from idea to working companion app.
