In this Gone DotNet episode, Jonathan Dick and Allan Ritchie tackle a massively buggy (and still closed source) face & voice recognition library, using AI and MAUI DevFlow to squash the bugs.

## What you'll learn

- **MAUI Sherpa SDK management** — a first look at Jon's still-unreleased .NET SDK management feature
- **A face & voice recognition library** — an early, buggy library and how the pair approach debugging it
- **AI in the loop** — bringing MAUI DevFlow in to help vibe out the bugs from a running app
- **Real-world agentic workflow** — the practical ups and downs of leaning on AI assistants mid-session

Watch the full episode for the live debugging session and candid takes on the current state of AI tooling.
