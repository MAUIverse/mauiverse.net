Gerald Versluis joins Barry Luijbregts on Code, Cloud & AI for a wide-ranging conversation that starts with mental health and Hyrox, then digs into the reality of shipping cross-platform .NET MAUI — and how AI is changing how the MAUI team itself works.

## What you'll learn

- **Why cross-platform is still hard** — what it takes to ship one C# codebase across iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows
- **Blazor Hybrid vs native UI** — when web-in-MAUI is the right flexibility tradeoff for .NET teams
- **AI inside the MAUI team** — Copilot for PR reviews, validating fixes, and interacting with running apps
- **MAUI Labs and Linux** — how agentic workflows are helping expand MAUI into new territory
- **Guardrails for agents** — custom instructions, skills, MCP servers, and self-verifying development patterns
- **Careers and education** — grounded takes on how AI changes developer productivity and what kids should study

Listen to the full episode for the practical patterns behind agentic development on a real product team.
