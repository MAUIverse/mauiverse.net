Combining a love of travel with UI practice, Leomaris Reyes replicates a boarding-pass design inspired by Dribbble. She breaks it into blocks and builds each part with clear code and step-by-step screenshots.

## What you'll learn

- **Structuring the ticket** — using Telerik RadBorder as the main rounded container with custom corner radius, padding, and margin
- **Laying out passenger details** — organizing airline, route, gate, seat, and booking info with nested Grids, Labels, and Line separators
- **The perforated edge effect** — placing Ellipse shapes to simulate the cut-out notches that make it read as a real ticket
- **Reusable layout techniques** — combining Shapes, Grid, and Border to recreate complex designs in a simple, maintainable way
