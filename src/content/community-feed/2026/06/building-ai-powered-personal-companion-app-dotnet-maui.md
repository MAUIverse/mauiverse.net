AI is reshaping how apps feel — more personalized, more intelligent, and more engaging. In this session, Gerald Versluis shows how to bring those experiences to a single cross-platform .NET MAUI app that runs across Windows, Android, iOS, and macOS.

## What you'll learn

- **Integrating AI into .NET MAUI:** wiring modern AI services into a cross-platform app
- **Conversational experiences:** adding natural language conversations to your UI
- **Smart features:** personalized recommendations, task assistance, and contextual insights
- **Architecture in practice:** best practices for structuring an AI-powered companion app
- **Live demonstrations** that take you from concept to a working intelligent experience

Whether you're a .NET developer modernizing your apps or an AI enthusiast curious about building intelligent user experiences, this ~1 hour session offers a practical starting point. Watch the full video to follow the demos and architecture end to end.
