Animations do more than decorate an app — they guide users and communicate what's happening. Leomaris Reyes shows how much you can achieve with .NET MAUI's simple animation primitives by building five creative heart effects.

## What you'll learn

- **Heart ECG monitor** — drawing a live, scrolling heartbeat line with GraphicsView, an `IDrawable`, and a dispatcher timer
- **Ripple pulse** — layering `ScaleTo` and `FadeTo` in a loop to radiate expanding rings from a beating heart
- **Blinking heart** — combining opacity and scale for a subtle, elegant pulse
- **Rotating heart** — a continuous `RotateTo` loop that doubles as a loading indicator
- **Jumping heart** — chaining `TranslateTo` calls with easing for a bouncy, looping motion

Read the full article for the XAML and code-behind behind each animation, ready to drop into your own .NET MAUI projects.
