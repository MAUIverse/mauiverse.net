Choosing your tech stack on day one shapes time to market, cost, and maintenance for years. Pavel Chuvak argues that .NET MAUI is the pragmatic, low-risk answer, and backs it with his team's real experience shipping and migrating cross-platform apps.

## What you'll learn

- **What .NET MAUI is** — Microsoft's open-source framework for native apps on iOS, Android, Windows, and macOS from one codebase
- **From Xamarin to MAUI** — why Xamarin's 2024 retirement makes MAUI the ecosystem's settled direction
- **The business advantages** — one codebase, one team, and one budget, with build-once features and fix-once bugs
- **Real cost impact** — how a single codebase can cut initial development costs by 30–40% versus two native projects
- **Why now** — choosing a technology that's already ahead rather than catching up
