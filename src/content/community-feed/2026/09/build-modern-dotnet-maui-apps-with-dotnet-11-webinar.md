Could .NET 11 finally solve some of .NET MAUI's biggest pain points? Syncfusion's show notes recap a webinar where Microsoft Principal Product Manager David Ortinau walked through what's arriving in .NET MAUI 11 — with full timestamps to the [recording](https://youtu.be/ExRWMGpm1rc).

## What you'll learn

- **CoreCLR becomes the runtime** — targeting .NET 11 runs MAUI on CoreCLR, with ReadyToRun and Native AOT deployment options for Android and their size/performance tradeoffs
- **XAML and Hot Reload** — more source generation, C# expressions in XAML instead of tiny converters, improved incremental Hot Reload, and `.NET Watch` across platforms
- **Better native experiences** — cross-platform long-press gestures, richer SwipeView styling, tab badges, Shell route templates, and map pin clustering
- **Security and Android polish** — passkey support for passwordless auth, Material 3 opt-in, safe-area/edge-to-edge layouts, and light/dark splash themes
- **Workflow and Syncfusion controls** — `.NET Run` device selection, expanded test support, AI-assisted upgrade/benchmark workflows (MAUI Dev Flow), and Toolkit controls like GridSplitter, InteractiveViewer, and chart tooltips
