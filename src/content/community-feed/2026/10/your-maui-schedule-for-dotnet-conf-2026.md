With four days of sessions, finding the MAUI content can take some effort. Tony has done the trawling and put it in date order, with all times in UK time.

The headline session is Gerald Versluis on what's new in .NET MAUI in .NET 11, including the move to CoreCLR, XAML source generation by default, C# expressions in XAML and incremental Hot Reload. Jonathan Peppers follows with `dotnet run`, `dotnet test` and `dotnet watch` for Android and iOS apps, which Tony expects to matter for VS Code users, automated builds and AI coding agents. Gerald's second session looks at giving AI "eyes and hands" through DevFlow, GitHub Copilot and an interactive mobile canvas.

The community days add a few talks with a different focus:

- **Production readiness:** Paul Usher on offline workflows, large datasets and hardware differences
- **Blazor Hybrid:** Shriram Sankaran on sharing Razor components between web and MAUI
- **Architecture:** Augustine Correa on a community platform built with Blazor, .NET MAUI, Aspire and Azure AI Foundry

Tony shares what he's most looking forward to and reminds readers that the event is free, with recordings expected afterwards. Use it as a shortlist before building your own calendar from the official agenda.
