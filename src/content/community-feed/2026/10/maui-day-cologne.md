Cologne is where Tony's own MAUI Day story began, so closing the 2026 series there clearly meant something. He describes a day of deep dives on crash-free apps, adaptable UIs, distributed coding tips and what's coming in .NET 11, followed by the hallway conversations that make these events worth attending.

His main takeaway is how the conversations felt compared with a few years ago:

- **Features:** the long-requested ones are arriving, with a big step change in .NET 11
- **Bugs:** the ones everyone has been complaining about are being fixed one by one
- **Performance:** it's no longer the worry it was
- **Tooling:** still a source of grumbles, though the team is improving what it can influence

It's a short, personal piece about community, from someone who lives in a remote part of the UK with very few local MAUI developers. If you've been to a MAUI Day, or wondered what they're like, it's worth a couple of minutes.
