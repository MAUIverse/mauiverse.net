Xamarin.Forms apps still waiting to move can be hard to size up. This very short Syncfusion video introduces a migration advisor that looks at your project offline and reports how ready it is for .NET MAUI.

According to the video's description, it helps you assess readiness, identify blockers, estimate the effort involved and find .NET MAUI alternatives for the controls you currently use. The clip is a teaser rather than a walkthrough, and the linked migration page has the details.

At around 40 seconds it's an easy watch, and a handy pointer if you're planning a Xamarin migration and want a first impression of the scope.
