Starting with a new framework is easier when someone tells you the mistakes are normal. Leomaris Reyes opens with that message and then offers advice from her own early experience with .NET MAUI.

The tips are about habits as much as technology. Understand what you're building and ask questions before you begin coding. Practice by recreating UI designs, starting simple and increasing the difficulty, and share what you learn along the way. Learn how the layouts differ, since choosing the right one can affect performance. She also suggests exploring third-party controls such as Telerik UI for .NET MAUI, and she lists community creators worth following, including Gerald Versluis, David Ortinau and Matt Goldman, plus the .NET MAUI Community Standups.

It's a good one to send to a colleague who's new to MAUI, or to revisit if your own practice has stalled.
