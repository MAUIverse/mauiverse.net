MAUI Day started the year in London in February and went on to Kraków in May, Skopje in September and Cologne in October. The newsletter gives each stop a short recap with a link to its archive page. London covered the future of .NET MAUI, Blazor Hybrid and XAML, Kraków ranged from Apple Watch integration to observability and .NET Aspire, and Skopje took place during WhatTheStack week and included the series' first workshop. Cologne finished the year at Microsoft's office, where a community survey suggested the mood has moved from what people wish MAUI had to what they're looking forward to.

The team keeps the focus on the people as much as the talks: the conversations over coffee, meeting someone you've only known online, and asking a MAUI team member the question you've been holding onto. They also say they'll keep events free where they can, thanks to sponsors.

The announcement is that MAUI Day London returns on 29 January 2027, with a proper conference venue this time, tickets on Eventbrite with an optional hoodie, and the first two speakers revealed. The call for papers is open on the MAUI Day website, and more 2027 dates will follow.
