.NET 11 is a big release for MAUI, and this .NET Conf session is the quickest way to see the whole picture. Gerald Versluis walks through the changes that matter most for existing apps: the runtime moving to CoreCLR, XAML source generation switched on by default, C# expressions directly in XAML, and XAML Incremental Hot Reload.

The session also covers Material 3 styling on Android, improvements to testing and the developer workflow, and where AI-assisted development fits into everyday MAUI work. The emphasis is practical: what each change means for the app you already have, and how to take advantage of it in your next release.

It's a good starting point if you're planning a move to .NET 11 and want a map of what to look at first, before you dig into the detailed sessions.
